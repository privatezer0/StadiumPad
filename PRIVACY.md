# StadiumPad privacy

StadiumPad is an independent tablet companion for a Helix Stadium you select on
your local network. It does not require a StadiumPad cloud account, include ads or
third-party analytics, or upload your presets to a developer-operated server.

## Local network and presets

Local Network permission allows Bonjour discovery and direct control/state
communication with your selected Stadium. Commands, current preset data and device
information travel between your iPad and that device. There is no Mac relay or
cloud preset sync in the current app.

Before edits, the app keeps saved/live native preset backups in its local Documents
folder. These may include personal preset names and settings. They remain on your
iPad unless you choose to export/share them, use Files or app file sharing, or back
up your device. The app retains the last connection address locally for reconnecting.
You can disconnect through the connection panel and manage your app data on iPadOS.

## TestFlight and feedback

Apple operates TestFlight and may provide beta diagnostics, crash information and
feedback under its terms and privacy practices. Feedback, screenshots or bug reports
you choose to submit can contain information you include. Public GitHub issues are
public; remove personal preset data, addresses and identifiers before posting.

Development-only diagnostic recording is explicitly enabled by developers and is
not an automatic cloud upload. Raw traces and personal presets are not distributed
with the source or beta app.

## Contact and changes

For privacy questions, contact the maintainer through the
[project repository](https://github.com/privatezer0/StadiumPad). Do not post private
preset data or credentials in a public issue. This policy describes the current
local-network beta; planned offline editing and later sync features are not shipped.
Any material change in data handling should be documented here before release.
